1.  Go to *Inventory > Operations > Sales Reservations* and click *New*.
2.  Choose the product, quantity, optionally the customer, and the
    *Expires in* duration.
3.  Click *Reserve*. The stock is reserved until the expiration date.
4.  Click *Release* to free the stock earlier. Expired reservations can be set
    back to draft and reserved again.

Warehouse users can open the linked transfer and use the *Manual Quants*
button (module `stock_quant_manual_assign`) to choose the exact quants.
